Our Story

Passion on Every Plate

Founded in 2018, The Grand Table was born from a desire to reimagine Indian fine dining — blending age-old recipes with modern culinary artistry.

Our team of 50+ award-winning chefs bring together flavours from every corner of India, creating dishes that are as visually stunning as they are delicious.
